Depth Street Replay

Your 2026-10-19 drive, 8.5–12.5 s. Each camera frame is lifted into 3D by an AI depth model (Depth Anything V2 Small), scaled to metres by the road the camera rig knows, and painted with its own video pixels.

Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom. Driver sits at the front camera; Chase and Top look at the car from outside.

Where cameras overlap, each spot comes from the camera that sees it sharpest; the road is snapped to the true road plane; neighbouring cameras meet on one surface and fade into each other; brightness is matched between cameras. Camera colours shows which camera drew what.

Gaps are what no camera saw. The white box is your car.
